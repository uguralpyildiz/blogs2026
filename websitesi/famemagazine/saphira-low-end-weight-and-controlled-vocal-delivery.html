Venus Anon Turns Softness Into Discipline on ‘gentle’

13 May 2026 • Voc Doc

Venus Anon returns with gentle, an intimate new single that turns anger, softness, and self-control into quiet tension.

Venus Anon - "gentle" (Music Video)
Venus Anon

Created during a three-month cabin stay while making her 2027 debut album, the song grew from guitar experiments into something inward, controlled, and quietly tense.

“

I’m a very angry person deep inside, but I choose peace every day. gentle is about how violence can feel tempting, and choosing softness anyway – Venus says.

Following Catharsis and cathy, quietly, gentle pushes Venus Anon's world into softer, still volatile territory, shaped by smart pop, emotional indie, electronic movement, and club culture.
